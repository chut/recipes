## Ingredients

- 1.5 lb chicken breast
- 1 stick butter
- 4–5 stalks of celery
- 1 bottle buffalo sauce
- 8 oz cream cheese (small block)
- 8 oz cheddar cheese
- 4 oz pepper jack cheese
- 4 green onions (greens only) or chives

## Instructions

1. Set Instant Pot to sauté high.
2. Cut butter stick in half — put one half in bottom of Instant Pot.
3. Add chicken to Instant Pot.
4. Dump all of hot sauce, cream cheese, and rest of butter on top (do not stir).
5. Set Instant Pot to pressure cook high for 15 minutes.
6. Halve each celery stalk down the middle and then chop finely.
7. Chop just the greens of green onions.
8. Turn on oven to 400°F.
9. Quick release and move chicken only to a large mixing bowl.
10. Set Instant Pot to sauté medium and stir regularly to reduce sauce.
11. While sauce is reducing, use a hand mixer (or stand mixer) to shred the chicken until all broken up into small stringy pieces.
12. Add chopped celery to the mixing bowl.
13. Shred and combine cheddar and jack cheese. Add ⅔ of cheese to mixing bowl and continue to mix until well combined.
14. When sauce is well reduced and smooth, start adding to the bowl with chicken, celery, and cheese. Add a little at a time — you may not need all of it depending on how runny you want the dip. (Usually about ¾ of it.)
15. Transfer mixture to an oven-safe dish and cover top with remaining cheese.
16. Bake in oven for 5–10 minutes until cheese is melted and bubbly. Can broil for a few minutes.
17. Remove from oven and sprinkle green onions (or chives) on top.
