## Ingredients

- 2 lb beef/pork/veal mix
- 1 small can black beans
- 1 small can kidney beans
- 1 large can crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup beef stock
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 5 cloves garlic
- 2 medium onions
- 1 shallot
- 4 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp oregano
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- Olive oil
- Flour (for spice paste)

## Instructions

1. Medium chop onion and shallot. Add to dutch oven with olive oil. Cook 5 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, in a separate large skillet add some oil and all the meat. Cook until browned.
3. When onions are translucent, add garlic and tomato paste. Brown paste for a few minutes. Deglaze with a small amount of broth. Add tomatoes and reduce heat.
4. When meat is done cooking, drain on paper towel and discard fat.
5. In a small bowl combine all dry ingredients: flour, chili powder, cumin, oregano, and brown sugar.
6. Add 2 tbsp oil to the meat pan. When hot, slowly add dry spice mix. Then add Worcestershire sauce.
7. When spices form a dark paste, add to dutch oven. Deglaze with a bit of broth to get all remaining spice from pan.
8. Add meat, both cans of beans, and cup of beef broth to dutch oven. Stir to evenly combine. Bring to a boil.
9. Reduce heat and cook covered for 3 hours.

## Notes

*Consider adding green pepper. Adjust seasoning to taste.*
