*Adapted from the NY Times recipe*

## Ingredients

- 1 lb bittersweet chocolate, chopped
- ½ lb (2 sticks) unsalted butter, cut into pieces
- 6 large eggs
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 tbsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- Cocoa powder or powdered sugar for dusting

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Butter and line a 9-inch springform pan with parchment.
2. Melt chocolate and butter together in a double boiler or microwave, stirring until smooth. Let cool slightly.
3. In a large bowl, whisk eggs and sugar until combined. Add vanilla and salt.
4. Fold the chocolate mixture into the egg mixture until fully combined.
5. Pour batter into prepared pan.
6. Bake for 35 minutes (for a 9-inch pan). The center should be just set — it will firm up as it cools.
7. Let cool completely in the pan before removing.
8. Dust with cocoa powder or powdered sugar before serving.

## Notes

*If using a 9-inch springform pan, multiply the base recipe by 1.25×. Cook for 35 minutes.*
