## Ingredients

- 3 chicken breasts
- 1½ cups celery, carrot, and onion, chopped
- 8–10 cloves garlic, chopped or made into a paste
- 1 box chicken broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- Cayenne, oregano, thyme, salt, pepper, garlic powder
- Fresh herbs: parsley, dill, thyme, chive, rosemary
- 1 lemon
- Calabrian chili paste
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 Parmesan rind
- Egg noodles
- Butter
- Potatoes, cubed
- White wine
- Neutral oil

## Instructions

1. Season chicken breasts.
2. Sear chicken in neutral oil. Remove and set aside.
3. Add butter to the same pan.
4. Add carrot, celery, and onion. Cook 5 minutes.
5. Add garlic and cook a couple more minutes.
6. Deglaze with white wine.
7. Add half the fresh herbs, bay leaves, and Calabrian chili paste. Sauté 1 minute.
8. Add the chicken broth, then the Parmesan rind and bay leaves.
9. Add potatoes.
10. Add back chicken and cook at a steady boil for 15–20 minutes.
11. Remove chicken and shred it.
12. Discard herb stems, bay leaves, and Parmesan rind.
13. Add cream, then lemon juice.
14. Add the rest of the fresh herbs.
15. Adjust spices to taste.
16. Add shredded chicken back and combine for a couple minutes.
17. Serve over buttered egg noodles, topped with Parmesan. Stir it all together into a soup.
