## Ingredients

- 3 lb Yukon Gold potatoes
- ¾ stick unsalted butter
- ½ cup cream
- ¼ cup milk
- 4 oz crème fraîche
- Chives, finely chopped

## Instructions

1. Peel potatoes, quarter, and put into cold water.
2. Cook 25 minutes or until fork tender.
3. In a separate pot, melt butter, add cream and milk. Warm over low heat until hot but not bubbling or boiling.
4. When potatoes are done, pass through a ricer.
5. Add the warm butter and milk mixture. Gently fold liquid into potatoes.
6. When close to fully incorporated, add crème fraîche and fold slowly. Use slow, gentle movements with the minimum amount of movement possible — do not overwork the potatoes.
7. Top with chives and serve immediately.
