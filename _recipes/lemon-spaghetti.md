*Adapted from Serious Eats Pasta al Limone*

## Ingredients

- 5 tbsp unsalted butter
- Zest of 1 lemon, plus fresh juice to taste
- 1 medium clove garlic, minced
- Kosher salt
- 1 lb fresh spaghetti (store-bought or homemade)
- 1 oz grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, plus more for serving
- Freshly ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. In a large skillet, melt butter over medium heat until foaming. Lower heat to low, add lemon zest and garlic, and cook, stirring, until fragrant, 1–2 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, in a medium pot of boiling salted water, cook pasta until just shy of al dente. Reserve about 1 cup of the starchy pasta water, then drain pasta.
3. Add pasta to butter mixture, along with ½ cup reserved pasta water. Increase heat to medium-high and cook, stirring and tossing to coat, until pasta is glazed in a satiny, emulsified sauce.
4. Add grated cheese and toss and stir rapidly until sauce thickens. If it becomes too tight and dry, add more pasta water to loosen.
5. Add lemon juice to taste, starting with 1 tablespoon, then adding more until desired tartness is reached. Season with salt and pepper.
6. Serve right away, topping each serving with additional grated lemon zest and cheese.

## Notes

*Use lots of lemon juice and butter — start low heat. Add cilantro and chopped bacon on top as optional garnishes. Use less cheese than you think.*
