*Adapted from the NY Times black bean chili recipe*

## Ingredients

- 1 lb dried black beans, soaked overnight
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 jalapeños, seeded and diced
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 4 cups vegetable or chicken broth
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Cilantro, sour cream, lime for serving

## Instructions

1. Soak black beans overnight. Drain and rinse.
2. Heat olive oil in a large dutch oven over medium heat.
3. Add onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
4. Add garlic and jalapeños. Cook 2 more minutes.
5. Add cumin, chili powder, and smoked paprika. Stir to coat.
6. Add drained beans, diced tomatoes, and broth.
7. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer covered for 1½–2 hours until beans are tender.
8. Season with salt and pepper.
9. Serve topped with cilantro, sour cream, and a squeeze of lime.
