## Ingredients

- 1 jar red curry paste
- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 3 cloves garlic
- ½ onion
- 1 can coconut milk
- 1 can coconut cream (solids)
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 red bell pepper
- 1 green bell pepper
- 1 small sweet potato
- 1 large carrot
- 1 lb chicken, thinly sliced
- Lime juice
- Neutral oil

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a pan.
2. Add onion, garlic, all the curry paste, and peanut butter. Cook for 3–5 minutes.
3. In a separate pan, cook the thinly sliced chicken over very high heat. Set aside.
4. Add peppers and carrots to the curry pan. Cook 1–2 minutes.
5. Add coconut cream solids and half the coconut milk. Stir and raise heat.
6. Mix cornstarch into the remaining coconut milk. Add to the pan along with fish sauce. Cook 3–4 minutes.
7. Add the cooked chicken.
8. Reduce until desired sauce thickness.
9. Finish with a squeeze of lime juice. Serve over rice.
