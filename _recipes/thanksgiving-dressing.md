## Ingredients

- 2 loaves French or Italian bread (about 12 cups cubed)
- 4 cups store-bought mixed flavored stuffing cubes
- 1 cup parsley
- Fresh herbs (sage, thyme, rosemary)
- Spices (white pepper, garlic powder)
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 1½ sticks butter
- 5 ribs celery
- 1 onion

## Instructions

1. Rip bread into cubes.
2. Dry in oven at 220°F, or overnight, or both.
3. Dice celery and onion. Cook over medium-high heat with one stick of butter. Add salt and pepper. Remove from heat when softened but still crisp.
4. In a large bowl, add cooked celery and onion, first 6 cups of bread cubes, store-bought cubes, parsley, fresh herbs, and other seasonings (white pepper, garlic powder).
5. Slowly add chicken broth and stir, breaking up bread and wetting it.
6. After adding about 2 cups of broth, add the rest of the bread. Keep adding broth until the stuffing has a good consistency — about 4 cups for 12+4 cups of bread.
7. Transfer to a greased baking dish. Dot the top with half a stick of butter and rosemary sprigs.
